Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

River Finance Corp ("River Finance," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and disclose it, and the choices available to you.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website at riverfinance.ca, applications for funding, and any other interactions you have with River Finance. We collect, use, and disclose personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to visitors to our website, applicants for funding, current and former customers, and any individual named in an application as a business owner, guarantor, or reference. Throughout this policy, "you" refers to any of these individuals.

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us.

Information you provide directly

  • Contact information submitted through our website, such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, and any message you send us.
  • Application information, if you apply for funding, such as your business's legal name, address, entity type, industry, and time in business, along with information about business owners, guarantors, or references. This may include Social Insurance Number, date of birth, or government-issued identification where required.
  • Credit information, where you authorize us to obtain a credit report or credit history as part of evaluating your application.

Information collected through a bank account connection

If you choose to connect your bank account through our website, this is done through a secure, third-party bank account connectivity provider. Your online banking credentials are entered directly with that provider and your financial institution; River Finance does not see, collect, or store your banking username or password. We may review the account and transaction data made available through this connection as part of evaluating your application.

Information collected automatically

Our website does not currently use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical information necessary to operate and secure the site, such as IP address and request timestamps. If we begin using analytics or similar technologies, we will update this section to reflect that.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • evaluate applications for funding and determine eligibility;
  • verify identity and prevent fraud;
  • communicate with you about an application, an account, or an inquiry you've made;
  • administer and service accounts for our customers;
  • comply with our legal and regulatory obligations; and
  • maintain and improve our website, products, and services.

Credit information

If you apply for funding, we may obtain, use, and disclose credit information about you and your business — including credit reports and credit history — to evaluate your application, assess risk, and administer your account, where you have authorized us to do so. The specific credit information obtained will depend on the nature of your application and will be described to you as part of that process.

Marketing Communications

From time to time, River Finance may contact you by email, text message, or other electronic means about products, services, or offers that may be of interest to you. Where required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation, we will obtain your consent before sending commercial electronic messages, and every such message will include a way to unsubscribe. Unsubscribing from marketing communications does not affect messages we send about an existing application or account, such as status updates or legal notices.

How We Share Your Information

River Finance does not sell or rent your personal information.

We share personal information with third-party service providers who perform functions on our behalf, including website hosting, email delivery, secure bank account connectivity, credit reporting, identity verification, and fraud prevention. These providers are authorized to use your information only to provide services to us and are required to protect it under appropriate contractual obligations.

We may also disclose personal information where required by law, such as in response to a court order or a lawful request from a regulator, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of River Finance, our customers, or the public. If River Finance is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

International Transfer of Information

Some of our service providers process personal information on servers located outside Canada. As a result, your personal information may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions and may be accessible to courts, regulators, or law enforcement authorities there. We take reasonable steps to require our service providers to protect personal information consistent with this Privacy Policy, regardless of where it is processed.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, meet our legal and regulatory obligations, and resolve any disputes. Retention periods vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

Accuracy of Information

We take reasonable steps to keep the personal information we hold accurate and up to date for the purposes for which it is used. If you believe information we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you can ask us to correct it using the contact information below.

Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will investigate and, where required by law, notify affected individuals and the applicable privacy regulator.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information, or withdraw your consent to our collection, use, or disclosure of your personal information. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request. If you withdraw consent for information necessary to provide a product or service, we may no longer be able to provide that product or service to you.

Privacy Complaints

If you have a question, concern, or complaint about how River Finance handles your personal information, please contact our Privacy Officer using the information below. We will review your complaint and respond in accordance with applicable privacy law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the applicable provincial privacy regulator.

Children's Privacy

Our website and products are directed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from individuals under the age of majority.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will post any revised version on this page and update the effective date above.
