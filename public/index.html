Working Capital

Working capital based on what your business earns — not what's on your balance sheet.

We provide a lump sum of capital in exchange for a fixed, agreed-upon portion of your future sales. It's a purchase of future receivables, not a loan — so there's no interest rate to calculate, just one clear total cost agreed before you sign.

River Finance provides a purchase of future receivables (revenue-based financing). This is not a traditional business loan.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this a loan?

No. River Finance provides revenue-based financing — a purchase of a fixed portion of your future sales. There's no interest rate to calculate; you're quoted one fixed total cost upfront.

How fast can I get funded?

Most approved applicants receive funds within 24 hours of accepting their offer, once all required documentation is confirmed.

What can I use the funding for?

Anything your business needs — payroll, inventory, equipment, marketing, expansion, or covering a short-term cash flow gap.

What if my business has less-than-perfect credit?

We weigh your business's cash flow and revenue more heavily than a personal credit score, so approval doesn't hinge on a perfect credit history.

How do repayments work?

Repayments are collected daily or weekly.
